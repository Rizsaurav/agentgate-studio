# Halcy 40 Owner's Manual

The Halcy 40 is Orrinby's lightest electric city bike, designed for short commutes and flat to rolling terrain. This manual covers the specifications, charging, riding modes and basic care. Read the safety section before your first ride.

## Specifications

| Item                         | Halcy 40                              |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Motor                        | 250 W rear hub motor                  |
| Maximum torque               | 45 Nm                                 |
| Battery                      | 400 Wh, integrated in the down tube   |
| Estimated range              | 40 to 70 km                           |
| Assisted top speed           | 25 km/h                               |
| Bike weight                  | 19.8 kg including battery             |
| Maximum rider and cargo load | 120 kg                                |
| Gears                        | 7-speed derailleur                    |
| Brakes                       | mechanical disc brakes, 160 mm rotors |
| Frame sizes                  | S, M, L                               |

The Halcy 40 uses a cadence sensor, so the motor responds to pedalling speed rather than pedalling force.

## Charging the battery

Use only the Orrinby Standard 4A charger supplied with the bike. A full charge from empty takes about 3.5 hours with the standard charger. Charging from 0 to 80% takes about 2 hours. The Halcy 40 does not support the Rapid 6A charger; connecting it shows error E23 on the display.

You can charge the battery on the bike or removed. To remove it, unlock the battery with the key and lift it out of the down tube. The charging port cover must be closed while riding.

## Riding modes

The Halcy 40 has three assist levels: Eco, Tour and Boost. Eco gives the longest range, around 70 km on flat roads. Boost gives about 40 km. Walk assist is activated by holding the minus button for 2 seconds and moves the bike at up to 6 km/h.

## Display

The handlebar display shows speed, assist level, battery percentage and trip distance. Hold the power button for 3 seconds to switch the bike on or off. The display turns off automatically after 10 minutes without movement. If an error code appears, see the Display Error Codes guide.

## Range factors

Range is reduced by cold weather, headwinds, hills, low tyre pressure and heavy loads. At temperatures below 5 °C, expect up to 30% less range.

## Care and maintenance

Keep tyres inflated to 3.5 to 5 bar. Clean the bike with a damp cloth; never use a pressure washer. Have the first service done after 500 km or 3 months, whichever comes first. The Halcy 40 receives firmware updates through the Orrinby Ride app.

## Safety

Always wear a helmet. Check the brakes before each ride. Do not ride through water deeper than 15 cm. The Halcy 40 is not designed for child seats or trailers.
