# Shipping and Delivery

Orrinby Cycleworks ships from its central warehouse in Brackwater. This page covers delivery speeds, costs, destinations and what to do if something goes wrong.

## Delivery options

| Option              | Delivery time            | Cost                                  |
| ------------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| Standard            | 3 to 5 business days     | EUR 6.95, free on orders over EUR 150 |
| Express             | 1 to 2 business days     | EUR 14.95                             |
| Bike freight        | 5 to 8 business days     | EUR 49 per bike                       |
| Showroom collection | Ready in 2 business days | Free                                  |

Express is only available for accessories and parts. Every complete bicycle, electric or not, ships by bike freight. Orders placed before 13:00 CET on a business day leave the warehouse the same day.

## Bicycles and e-bikes

Standard bicycles are shipped 90% assembled: you attach the handlebar, pedals and front wheel with the tools in the box. E-bikes are shipped 95% assembled with the battery packed separately in a certified dangerous-goods box. E-bikes ship partially charged, at roughly 30% battery, and must be fully charged before the first ride.

The bike freight carrier calls you to book a two-hour delivery slot. Someone aged 18 or over must sign for the delivery. If nobody is home, the carrier tries once more; after a second missed delivery the bike goes back to Brackwater and a redelivery fee of EUR 30 applies.

## Where we deliver

We deliver to Germany, the Netherlands, Belgium, Luxembourg, Austria, Denmark, Sweden and the United Kingdom. Accessories can also be shipped to Norway and Switzerland, but complete bikes cannot. We do not ship e-bikes or loose batteries to islands that are only reachable by air, because lithium batteries above 100 Wh cannot travel as air cargo.

UK orders are shipped Delivered Duty Paid, so the price at checkout already includes import VAT and duties.

## Tracking

You receive a tracking link by email as soon as the parcel leaves the warehouse. Bike freight tracking updates once a day; parcel tracking updates in real time.

## Damaged or missing parcels

Check your delivery before signing. If the box is visibly damaged, write "damaged" on the carrier's form. Report damage to us within 48 hours of delivery, with photos of the box and the product.

If a parcel has not arrived 10 business days after the expected date, contact Customer Care. Lost parcel claims must be raised within 21 days of the expected delivery date; after that, we cannot open an investigation with the carrier. Once a parcel is confirmed lost, we send a replacement or refund at your choice.

## Address changes

You can change the delivery address free of charge until the order leaves the warehouse. After dispatch, address changes for parcels cost EUR 5 and are not possible for bike freight.
