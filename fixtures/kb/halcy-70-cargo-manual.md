# Halcy 70 Cargo Owner's Manual

The Halcy 70 Cargo is Orrinby's long-tail electric cargo bike. It carries children, groceries or work equipment, and has the largest battery and the strongest motor in the Halcy range.

## Specifications

| Item                   | Halcy 70 Cargo                                |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Motor                  | 250 W mid-drive cargo motor                   |
| Maximum torque         | 85 Nm                                         |
| Battery                | 700 Wh, mounted under the rear deck           |
| Estimated range        | 70 to 120 km                                  |
| Assisted top speed     | 25 km/h                                       |
| Bike weight            | 34.6 kg including battery                     |
| Maximum total payload  | 200 kg, including rider                       |
| Maximum rear deck load | 80 kg                                         |
| Gears                  | 5-speed internal gear hub with belt drive     |

## Charging the battery

The Halcy 70 Cargo ships with the Orrinby Standard 4A charger. A full charge from empty takes about 6.5 hours. With the optional Rapid 6A charger, a full charge takes about 4 hours and 0 to 80% takes about 2.5 hours. An optional second battery (also 700 Wh) can be mounted in the front frame bay, doubling the range; both batteries charge one after the other through the same port.

## Carrying passengers

The rear deck accepts up to two Orrinby child seats, for children aged 1 to 7 years and up to 22 kg each. A passenger bench for one older child up to 40 kg is also available. The included wheel guards must be fitted whenever children ride on the deck. Never carry passengers on the front frame bay.

## Riding modes

Four assist levels are available: Eco, Tour, Cargo and Boost. Cargo mode increases low-speed torque for starting on hills with a full load. Walk assist moves the bike at up to 6 km/h, which is useful for ramps. The parking brake on the left handlebar locks the rear wheel.

## Stand and parking

The centre stand is rated for loading the bike with up to 80 kg on the deck. Always use the parking brake when children get on or off.

## Range factors

With a full 200 kg load in hilly terrain, range may drop to about 45 km. Cold weather below 5 °C reduces range by up to 30%.

## Care and maintenance

Keep tyres inflated to 2.5 to 4 bar. The belt drive needs no lubrication; check belt tension every 1,000 km. The first service is due after 500 km or 3 months, whichever comes first.

## Safety

Practise riding without a load before carrying children. Children must always wear a helmet and be strapped into the child seat. Do not ride through water deeper than 20 cm.
