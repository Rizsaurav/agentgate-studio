# Returns and Refunds Policy

This policy explains how to return products bought from Orrinby Cycleworks, either from our online store or from one of our three showrooms. It applies to orders delivered on or after 1 March 2026. Earlier orders follow the policy that was published at the time of purchase.

## Return windows by product type

Different products have different return windows, counted from the day the order is delivered (or collected in a showroom):

- **Standard bicycles** (non-electric, including the Tessel city bike): you can return a standard bicycle within 30 days of delivery.
- **Electric bicycles** (the Halcy 40, Halcy 52 and Halcy 70 Cargo): e-bikes must be returned within 14 days of delivery, and the odometer must show less than 50 km.
- **Accessories and parts** (helmets, locks, lights, racks, panniers, chargers): accessories can be returned within 60 days of delivery if unused and in original packaging.
- **Clearance items** (anything marked "Final Clearance" at checkout): clearance items can be returned within 7 days and are refunded as store credit only.
- **Custom-built bicycles** made to a customer's measurements cannot be returned unless faulty.

Helmets that have been worn cannot be returned for hygiene and safety reasons, even within the accessory window.

## Condition requirements

Returned bikes must be clean, undamaged and complete, with all reflectors, manuals and keys. Electric bicycles are inspected at our Brackwater service centre. If an e-bike is returned with more than 50 km on the odometer, we may refuse the return or treat it as a used trade-in under the Trade-In Programme.

## Restocking fees

There is no restocking fee for standard bicycles or accessories. A restocking fee of 10% of the purchase price applies to returned e-bikes that are not faulty. The fee covers battery testing, firmware reset and re-certification. Faulty e-bikes returned within the warranty terms are never charged a restocking fee.

## How to start a return

1. Sign in to your Orrinby account and open **Orders**.
2. Select the item and choose **Start a return**.
3. Pick a reason and a collection date.

For standard bicycles and accessories we email a prepaid label. For e-bikes, which must travel by freight because of the battery, we book a courier collection. Return shipping is free if the product is faulty or we sent the wrong item. Otherwise, return shipping costs EUR 35 for a standard bicycle and EUR 75 for an e-bike. Accessory returns are always free to ship.

## Refund timing

Once the return arrives, our inspection team checks it within 3 business days. Refunds are issued within 5 business days after the inspection is passed, to the original payment method. Bank transfer refunds can take a further 2 to 4 business days to appear, depending on your bank. If you paid with Brevlin Finance instalments, the refund cancels the remaining instalments and returns what you have already paid.

## Exchanges

If you want a different frame size or colour, choose **Exchange** instead of **Return**. Exchanges follow the same windows as returns, but no restocking fee applies to an e-bike exchanged for another e-bike of equal or higher price. Exchanges are subject to stock availability; if the replacement is out of stock for more than 21 days, we refund you instead.

## Showroom purchases

Items bought in the Brackwater, Elmsby or Norrhaven showrooms can be returned to any showroom or through the online process. Showroom staff can approve standard bicycle and accessory returns on the spot. E-bike returns are always sent to the Brackwater service centre for inspection before a refund is approved.
