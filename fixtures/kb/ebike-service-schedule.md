# E-Bike Service Schedule

Regular servicing keeps Halcy e-bikes safe and protects the warranty. This schedule applies to the Halcy 40, Halcy 52 and Halcy 70 Cargo. The non-electric Tessel has its own schedule in its manual.

## Service intervals

| Service        | When                                               | Typical duration |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| First service  | after 500 km or 3 months, whichever comes first    | 45 minutes       |
| Annual service | every 2,000 km or 12 months, whichever comes first | 90 minutes       |
| Major service  | every 8,000 km or 3 years                          | 3 hours          |

Missing the first service does not void the warranty, but damage caused by loose bolts or untrued wheels that the first service would have caught is not covered.

## What each service includes

**First service**: re-tighten all bolts to torque, true the wheels, adjust the brakes and gears, check spoke tension, update all firmware.

**Annual service**: everything in the first service, plus a brake fluid check (hydraulic brakes), brake pad inspection, chain or belt wear measurement, battery health test, motor diagnostic readout, and a check of all electrical connectors.

**Major service**: everything in the annual service, plus hydraulic brake bleed, replacement of the chain and cassette (Halcy 40 and Halcy 52) or belt (Halcy 70 Cargo), wheel bearing service, and suspension seatpost service.

Care Basic includes one annual service per year. Care Plus includes two services per year and covers brake pads and chains.

## Booking a service

Book online under **Account > My bikes > Book a service**, or by phone. Service appointments are available Monday to Saturday in all three showrooms and at the Brackwater service centre. A free replacement bike is available during services that take longer than one day, if you have Care Plus.

## Firmware updates

Firmware updates are installed at every service. You can also install them yourself through the Orrinby Ride app, which checks for updates every time the bike connects. Do not switch the bike off during an update, which takes up to 8 minutes.

## Brake pads and wear parts

Brake pads should be replaced when the pad material is thinner than 1 mm. Halcy 70 Cargo pads typically last 1,500 to 2,500 km because of the higher weight; Halcy 40 and 52 pads last 2,500 to 4,000 km. A chain should be replaced at 0.5% elongation.

## Winter care

Road salt speeds up wear. In winter, rinse the bike with low-pressure water after salty rides, dry the chain and apply lubricant. Bring the battery indoors overnight when temperatures drop below 0 °C.
