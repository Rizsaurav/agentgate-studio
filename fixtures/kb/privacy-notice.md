# Privacy Notice

Orrinby Cycleworks B.V. is the data controller for personal data collected through our website, showrooms, the Orrinby Ride app and the Orrinby Fleet API. This notice explains what we collect, why, and for how long.

## What we collect

- **Account and order data**: name, email, phone, delivery and billing addresses, order history.
- **Payment data**: we store only the last four digits and expiry date of your card. Full card numbers are handled by our payment processor and never reach our systems.
- **Ride telemetry** from connected e-bikes: GPS location, speed, assist level, battery temperature and charge state.
- **Support data**: emails, chat transcripts and call notes.
- **Showroom CCTV**: recorded for security in all three showrooms.

## Why we use it

We use order data to deliver products and provide warranty service; telemetry to show ride statistics, detect battery faults and improve range estimates; and support data to resolve your requests. We do not sell personal data to anyone, and we do not use ride telemetry for advertising.

## Retention periods

| Data                      | How long we keep it                                                      |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Order and invoice records | 7 years, to meet tax law                                                 |
| Ride telemetry            | 18 months, then aggregated and anonymised                                |
| Support chats and emails  | 24 months after the case is closed                                       |
| Showroom CCTV             | 30 days                                                                  |
| Marketing preferences     | until you withdraw consent                                               |
| Deleted accounts          | personal data removed within 30 days, except records we must keep by law |

## Telemetry controls

You can switch off ride telemetry at any time in the Orrinby Ride app under **Settings > Privacy > Share ride data**. Switching it off stops GPS collection immediately. Battery health data is still collected while the bike is under warranty, because it is needed to assess battery claims; it is kept without location data.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete and export your personal data, and to object to certain processing. Send requests to our Data Protection Officer at privacy@orrinby.example or through **Account > Privacy**. We answer data subject requests within 30 days. For complex requests, we may extend this by up to two further months and will tell you why.

## Sharing with others

We share data only with processors that help us run the business: our payment processor, Brevlin Finance (if you apply for financing), our freight carriers, and our cloud hosting provider. All processors are bound by data processing agreements. Customer data is hosted in data centres in the European Union.

## Children

Our services are not intended for children under 16. Child seat accessories for the Halcy 70 Cargo do not collect any data about passengers.

## Changes and complaints

We notify registered users by email at least 30 days before any material change to this notice. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
