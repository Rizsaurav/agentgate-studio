# HR Handbook: Expenses Policy

This chapter explains which business expenses Orrinby reimburses and how to claim them. All claims are submitted in Spendrel, our expense tool.

## General rules

Spend company money as if it were your own. Expenses must be necessary for your work and reasonable in price. Personal expenses, fines, and alcohol (except at approved team events) are never reimbursed.

## Submitting a claim

Submit expenses in Spendrel within 60 days of the expense date. Claims older than 60 days are rejected unless the finance team approves an exception. Attach a receipt for every expense above EUR 25. For expenses of EUR 25 or less, a description is enough.

Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary if submitted before the 20th of the month.

## Approval limits

| Amount per claim       | Approver                    |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------- |
| up to EUR 1,000        | your line manager           |
| EUR 1,000 to EUR 5,000 | your department director    |
| above EUR 5,000        | the Chief Financial Officer |

Approvers cannot approve their own expenses.

## Meals

When working away from your normal office, meals are reimbursed up to EUR 45 per day in your own country and up to EUR 60 per day abroad. Client dinners are reimbursed up to EUR 80 per person, and the names of attendees must be listed in the claim.

## Home office equipment

New employees who work remotely at least two days a week can claim a one-time home office budget of EUR 500 for a desk, chair or monitor. Laptops, headsets and keyboards are provided by IT and are not bought on expenses. The monthly internet allowance is described in the Remote Work chapter, not here.

## Training and books

Each employee has a learning budget of EUR 1,200 per calendar year for courses, conferences and books. Conference travel counts against the travel policy, not the learning budget. Unused learning budget does not carry over.

## Mobile phones

Employees on an on-call rotation receive a company phone. Others may claim up to EUR 20 per month towards a personal phone contract if their role requires being reachable.

## Corporate cards

Managers and frequent travellers receive an Orrinby corporate card. Card transactions still need a receipt uploaded to Spendrel within 30 days. Personal use of the corporate card is not allowed, even if you pay it back.

## Mistakes and misuse

If you make a mistake in a claim, tell the finance team; honest mistakes are simply corrected. Deliberate misuse of expenses is treated as gross misconduct.
