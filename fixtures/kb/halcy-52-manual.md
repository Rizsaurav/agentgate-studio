# Halcy 52 Owner's Manual

The Halcy 52 is Orrinby's all-round electric touring bike. It has a mid-drive motor and a larger battery than the Halcy 40, making it suitable for longer commutes, hills and weekend tours.

## Specifications

| Item                         | Halcy 52                             |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Motor                        | 250 W mid-drive motor                |
| Maximum torque               | 70 Nm                                |
| Battery                      | 520 Wh, integrated in the down tube  |
| Estimated range              | 55 to 95 km                          |
| Assisted top speed           | 25 km/h                              |
| Bike weight                  | 23.4 kg including battery            |
| Maximum rider and cargo load | 130 kg                               |
| Gears                        | 10-speed derailleur                  |
| Brakes                       | hydraulic disc brakes, 180 mm rotors |
| Frame sizes                  | S, M, L, XL                          |

The Halcy 52 uses a torque sensor, so assistance increases the harder you pedal.

## Charging the battery

The Halcy 52 ships with the Orrinby Standard 4A charger. A full charge from empty takes about 5 hours with the standard charger, and 0 to 80% takes about 3 hours. The Halcy 52 also supports the optional Rapid 6A charger, which charges the battery fully in about 3.5 hours.

The battery can be charged on or off the bike. Charging is only possible when the battery temperature is between 0 °C and 40 °C.

## Riding modes

The Halcy 52 has four assist levels: Eco, Tour, Sport and Boost. In Eco the range is about 95 km in good conditions; in Boost it drops to about 55 km. Walk assist works as on all Halcy models, up to 6 km/h. Since firmware 2.8, the Halcy 52 also offers an Auto mode that adjusts assistance to the gradient.

## Display and lights

The colour display shows speed, assist level, battery percentage, range estimate and cadence. Hold the power button for 3 seconds to turn the bike on. The integrated front light produces 70 lux and switches on automatically in low light. The rear light has a brake-light function.

## Range factors

Range depends on temperature, terrain, tyre pressure, wind and total weight. A rider carrying 20 kg of luggage can expect around 15% less range.

## Accessories

The rear rack carries up to 25 kg and accepts Orrinby panniers and a child seat for children up to 22 kg. Trailers up to 40 kg may be towed with the Orrinby trailer hitch.

## Care and maintenance

Keep tyres inflated to 3 to 4.5 bar. The first service is due after 500 km or 3 months, whichever comes first. Keep the chain clean and lubricated, especially in winter. The mid-drive motor is sealed and must not be opened by the owner.

## Safety

Always wear a helmet, check the brakes before each ride, and do not ride through water deeper than 20 cm.
