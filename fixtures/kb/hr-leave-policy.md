# HR Handbook: Leave Policy

This chapter explains annual leave, public holidays, sick leave, parental leave and other leave for Orrinby employees. Where local law gives more, local law applies. Questions go to the People team.

## Annual leave by country

Annual leave depends on your employing entity:

| Country        | Annual leave (full-time)              |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Germany        | 28 working days                       |
| Netherlands    | 25 working days                       |
| United Kingdom | 26 working days plus public holidays  |
| Sweden         | 30 working days                       |
| Denmark        | 25 working days plus 5 extra holidays |

Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their contracted hours. Employees receive one additional day of leave after 5 years of service, and two additional days after 10 years.

## Booking leave

Book leave in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days. Your manager approves or declines within 5 working days. Leave over the company change freeze (15 December to 5 January) is encouraged, as deploys are restricted then.

## Carry-over

You may carry over up to 5 unused days into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March, or they expire. Statutory minimum leave may have different rules in your country; the HR portal always shows the correct balance.

## Public holidays

You receive the public holidays of the country you are employed in. If you work in another country temporarily, your home country's holidays still apply.

## Sick leave

Report sickness to your manager before 10:00 on the first day. You may self-certify sickness for up to 3 calendar days. From the fourth day, a doctor's note is required (in the Netherlands, the company doctor contacts you instead). Sick pay continues at full salary for up to 6 weeks, after which statutory rules apply.

## Parental leave

The primary caregiver receives 20 weeks of parental leave at full pay. The secondary caregiver receives 6 weeks at full pay, to be taken within the first 12 months after birth or adoption. This is in addition to any statutory parental leave. Notify the People team at least 8 weeks before the planned start.

## Other leave

- **Bereavement**: 5 days for a partner, child or parent; 2 days for other close relatives.
- **Moving house**: 1 day per year.
- **Volunteering**: 2 days per year for registered charities.
- **Unpaid leave**: up to 3 months after 2 years of service, with manager and People team approval.

## Leave and on-call

You cannot be scheduled for on-call while on annual leave. If you are paged while on leave by mistake, you receive a replacement day.
