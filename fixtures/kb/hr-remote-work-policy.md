# HR Handbook: Remote Work Policy

Orrinby supports hybrid work. This chapter explains how many days you may work remotely, from where, and what support you receive.

## Hybrid working

Office-based employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Teams agree on at least one shared office day per week, usually Tuesday or Wednesday. Showroom and service-centre staff work on site, because their roles require it.

## Fully remote roles

Some engineering and support roles are fully remote. Fully remote employees must live in a country where Orrinby has an employing entity (Germany, the Netherlands, the United Kingdom, Sweden or Denmark). Fully remote employees visit the Brackwater office for the company gathering twice a year; travel is paid under the travel policy.

## Working from abroad

Employees may work from another country for up to 30 working days per calendar year, with no more than 15 consecutive working days. Request working from abroad in the HR portal at least 3 weeks in advance. Some countries are excluded for tax or security reasons; the HR portal shows the current list. Working from abroad is not allowed while on call.

## Core hours

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone. Meetings should be scheduled within core hours where possible. Outside core hours, you choose when to work, as long as your team's needs are met.

## Equipment and allowances

IT provides a laptop, headset and keyboard. Remote and hybrid employees can claim the one-time home office budget described in the Expenses Policy. In addition, employees who work remotely at least two days a week receive an internet allowance of EUR 30 per month, paid with salary.

## Security at home

Use the company VPN when accessing internal systems. Lock your screen when you step away, and do not let family members use your work laptop. Confidential customer data must never be printed at home. Public Wi-Fi may only be used with the VPN switched on.

## Health and safety

Set up your home workspace according to the ergonomic checklist in the HR portal. If you develop pain or discomfort, the People team can arrange an ergonomic assessment at no cost.

## Changing your arrangement

To change from hybrid to fully remote, or to move to a different city, discuss it with your manager and the People team. Approval depends on your role, your team's needs and the employment rules of the new location. Relocation costs for a voluntary move are not covered.
