# Battery and Charger Care Guide

This guide applies to all Halcy e-bike batteries (400 Wh, 520 Wh and 700 Wh) and to the two Orrinby chargers. Good battery habits extend the life of the battery and protect your warranty.

## The two chargers

| Charger             | Output    | Compatible models                |
| ------------------- | --------- | -------------------------------- |
| Standard 4A charger | 42 V, 4 A | all Halcy models                 |
| Rapid 6A charger    | 42 V, 6 A | Halcy 52 and Halcy 70 Cargo only |

The Rapid 6A charger is sold separately for EUR 129. The Standard 4A charger costs EUR 79 as a replacement. Using any non-Orrinby charger voids the battery warranty.

## Charger light patterns

The charger has a single LED:

- **Steady red**: charging.
- **Steady green**: fully charged, or no battery connected.
- **Blinking red**: fault. Unplug the charger for 1 minute and try again. If it keeps blinking, the charger must be replaced.
- **Alternating red and green**: the battery is too cold or too hot to charge.

## Charging temperatures

Charge the battery only at temperatures between 0 °C and 40 °C. The ideal charging temperature is between 10 °C and 25 °C. You can ride at temperatures from -10 °C to 40 °C. After riding in freezing weather, let the battery warm up indoors for an hour before charging.

## Storage

If you will not ride for more than 4 weeks, store the battery at 40% to 60% charge in a dry place between 10 °C and 20 °C. Check the charge every 3 months and top up to 50% if needed. Never store a battery completely empty: a battery left at 0% for several months can enter deep discharge and become unusable, which is not covered by the warranty.

## Everyday habits

You do not need to run the battery empty before charging. Partial charges are fine and do not shorten battery life. If you ride short distances daily, charging to 80% instead of 100% further reduces wear; the Orrinby Ride app can stop charging at 80% automatically when the bike is connected via Bluetooth.

## Battery lifespan

A Halcy battery keeps at least 70% of its capacity for 800 full charge cycles under normal use. One full cycle equals charging 100% of the capacity, so two charges from 50% to 100% count as one cycle. The Orrinby Ride app shows the cycle count and estimated health under **Bike > Battery**.

## Replacement batteries

Replacement batteries cost EUR 549 for the 400 Wh battery, EUR 649 for the 520 Wh battery and EUR 799 for the 700 Wh battery. Old batteries are recycled free of charge at any showroom.

## Transport

Remove the battery before carrying the bike on a car rack. Lithium batteries above 100 Wh cannot be taken on passenger flights or shipped as air cargo.
