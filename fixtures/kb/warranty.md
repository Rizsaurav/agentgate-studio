# Warranty Terms

Orrinby Cycleworks warrants its products against defects in materials and workmanship for the periods below. The warranty applies to the original owner and starts on the date of delivery. Proof of purchase is required for every claim.

## Coverage periods

| Component                              | Warranty period                                          |
| -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Aluminium frames (all models)          | 10 years                                                 |
| Steel frames (Tessel city bike)        | 15 years                                                 |
| Forks, handlebars, stems and seatposts | 5 years                                                  |
| E-bike motors                          | 3 years                                                  |
| E-bike batteries                       | 2 years or 800 full charge cycles, whichever comes first |
| E-bike displays and controllers        | 2 years                                                  |
| Paint and finish                       | 2 years                                                  |
| Accessories sold by Orrinby            | 1 year                                                   |

A battery is considered defective under warranty if its usable capacity falls below 70% of the rated capacity within the warranty period. Normal capacity loss above that threshold is expected ageing and is not a defect.

## What is not covered

The warranty does not cover:

- normal wear parts such as tyres, inner tubes, brake pads, chains, cassettes, grips and saddles;
- damage from crashes, misuse, neglect, or improper assembly by a third party;
- bikes used for rental, courier or other commercial use, unless they are covered by a Care Fleet subscription;
- racing, jumps or downhill riding;
- batteries charged with a non-Orrinby charger;
- water damage caused by pressure washing.

Modifying the motor controller or installing third-party firmware voids the motor, battery and display warranties immediately.

## Making a claim

Claims are made through the warranty portal in your Orrinby account, under **Support > Warranty claim**. Attach photos of the problem, the frame serial number (stamped under the bottom bracket) and, for e-bikes, a diagnostic report exported from the Orrinby Ride app. Our Warranty Desk responds to every claim within 3 business days.

If the claim is accepted, we repair or replace the defective part at our choice. Replacement parts may be refurbished to an as-new standard. If a frame model is no longer produced, we replace it with the closest current model.

## Shipping for warranty claims

For accepted claims, we pay collection and return shipping within the countries we deliver to. If the claim is rejected after inspection, a diagnostic fee of EUR 45 applies and the product is returned at the customer's cost.

## Warranty transfer

The warranty is not transferable when a bike is sold privately, with one exception: the frame warranty transfers to a second owner if the bike is registered to them in the Orrinby Ride app within 30 days of the sale. Motor and battery warranties never transfer.

## Repairs after the warranty

After the warranty ends, our service centres still repair Orrinby products at standard workshop rates of EUR 68 per hour. Replacement batteries for out-of-warranty e-bikes are sold at a 15% discount to customers with an active Care Plus subscription.

## Legal rights

These terms are in addition to your statutory rights as a consumer, which are not affected.
