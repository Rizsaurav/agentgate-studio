# Payments and Financing

This page explains how to pay for Orrinby Cycleworks orders, how financing works and what happens when a payment fails.

## Accepted payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express credit and debit cards, SEPA bank transfer, and instalment financing through our partner Brevlin Finance. Showrooms also accept contactless payments. We do not accept cash for orders above EUR 2,000, cheques, or cryptocurrency.

Prices are shown in EUR for EU countries, in GBP for the United Kingdom, and in SEK for Sweden. You are charged in the currency shown at checkout.

## Card payments

When you place an order by card, we place an authorisation hold for the full amount. The card is charged when the order is dispatched. If an item is out of stock, the hold is released for that item. Authorisation holds that are not captured expire after 7 days, and we request a new authorisation if the order has not shipped by then.

All card payments use 3-D Secure verification. If verification fails three times, the order is cancelled automatically and you need to place it again.

## Bank transfer

Choose SEPA bank transfer at checkout to receive our account details and a payment reference. Orders paid by bank transfer are held for 7 days while we wait for the money. If the payment has not arrived after 7 days, the order is cancelled. Always include the payment reference; transfers without one take up to 10 business days to match.

## Financing with Brevlin Finance

Brevlin Finance offers 0% interest financing over 12 months on orders over EUR 1,000. Longer terms of 24 or 36 months are available at 7.9% APR. Financing is only available to residents of Germany, the Netherlands and Austria who are 18 or older, and is subject to a credit check by Brevlin Finance. The first instalment is collected 30 days after dispatch.

You can settle the remaining balance early at any time without a fee.

## Failed and declined payments

For one-off orders, a declined card simply stops the order; nothing is charged. For subscriptions (see the Orrinby Care subscription terms), failed payments are retried automatically on days 3, 5 and 7 after the due date. If all retries fail, the subscription is suspended.

## Chargebacks and disputes

Please contact Customer Care before opening a dispute with your bank; most issues are resolved within 2 business days. If a chargeback is opened while a return is already in progress, we pause the return until the bank decides. Accounts with more than two chargebacks in 12 months can only pay by bank transfer.

## Invoices and VAT

A VAT invoice is emailed when the order is dispatched and is always available under **Orders** in your account. Business customers can add a VAT number at checkout; reverse charge applies to valid EU VAT numbers outside Germany. Invoices cannot be re-issued to a different company name more than 90 days after the invoice date.
