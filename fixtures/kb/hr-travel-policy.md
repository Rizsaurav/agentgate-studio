# HR Handbook: Business Travel Policy

This chapter applies to all business travel by Orrinby employees and contractors. Travel is booked through the Waypost travel desk, which applies the rules below automatically.

## Before you travel

Business travel must be approved by your manager in Waypost before booking. Book at least 14 days before departure where possible; last-minute bookings need a short justification. For travel outside Europe, check visa requirements with the People team at least 6 weeks ahead.

## Choosing how to travel

Rail is the default for journeys that take less than 5 hours by train, even if a flight is cheaper. For longer journeys, flights are allowed.

| Journey                    | Class                                               |
| -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Train, any length          | second class; first class for journeys over 3 hours |
| Flights under 6 hours      | economy                                             |
| Flights of 6 hours or more | premium economy                                     |

Business class is never booked, except on medical grounds with People team approval.

## Hotels

Hotels are booked through Waypost. The nightly cap is EUR 160 in most cities, and EUR 230 in London, Paris, Amsterdam and Stockholm. Staying with friends instead of a hotel is allowed; you may then claim EUR 30 per night for a thank-you gift.

## Cars and mileage

Rental cars are allowed only when no reasonable public transport exists or when carrying equipment, such as demo bikes. Book the smallest suitable car. When you use your own car for business, mileage is reimbursed at EUR 0.30 per km. Riding an Orrinby e-bike to a business meeting is reimbursed at EUR 0.20 per km, regardless of who owns the e-bike.

## Meals while travelling

Meal limits are the same as in the Expenses Policy: EUR 45 per day in your own country and EUR 60 per day abroad.

## Weekends and private travel

You may extend a business trip for private reasons if the extra cost to Orrinby is zero. Hotel nights for the private part are always paid by you.

## Safety

Register every trip in Waypost so the company can reach you in an emergency. Waypost's 24-hour emergency line is available to all travellers. If a trip is disrupted, rebook through Waypost; additional costs are covered.

## Travel time

Travel on a weekend or public holiday that is required by the company gives you a day off in lieu, to be taken within 3 months.

## Sustainability

Orrinby aims to cut business travel emissions by 40% by 2028. Teams receive a yearly carbon budget, and video calls should be preferred for meetings under 2 hours.
