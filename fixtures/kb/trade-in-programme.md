# Trade-In Programme

The Orrinby Trade-In Programme lets you exchange your old bicycle for credit towards a new Orrinby bike. It is run by our Brackwater service centre together with the showroom teams.

## Eligibility

We accept bicycles of any brand that are less than 8 years old, roadworthy, and have an identifiable frame serial number. Electric bicycles must have a working battery that still holds at least 60% of its original capacity. We do not accept children's bikes, folding bikes with frame damage, or bikes that have been reported stolen.

Trade-in credit can only be used against a new complete bicycle, not against accessories, Orrinby Care subscriptions or clearance items.

## How much credit you get

The credit depends on the bike type and its condition grade:

| Bike type            | Maximum trade-in credit |
| -------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Standard bicycle     | EUR 400                 |
| Electric bicycle     | EUR 900                 |
| Orrinby Halcy e-bike | EUR 1,100               |

Condition grades are A (like new), B (light wear) and C (heavy wear). Grade A receives the full value from our price guide, grade B receives 70%, and grade C receives 40%. Bikes that need repairs costing more than the credit amount are declined.

## Getting a quote

Request a quote online under **Trade-In > Get a quote**. Enter the brand, model, year and photos from both sides. You receive an indicative quote within 2 business days. The quote is valid for 30 days. The final value is confirmed after a physical inspection at a showroom or the Brackwater service centre.

## Handing over your bike

You can hand over your bike in any showroom when you collect your new bike, or have it picked up by our freight carrier when your new bike is delivered. Pick-up at delivery costs EUR 20, which is deducted from the credit. You must bring the keys, charger and battery for an e-bike trade-in; missing chargers reduce the credit by EUR 60.

## Data on traded-in e-bikes

If you trade in an Orrinby Halcy e-bike, remove it from your Orrinby Ride account before handing it over. Our technicians perform a factory reset that deletes stored rides and pairing data.

## Combining with other offers

Trade-in credit can be combined with Brevlin Finance financing: the credit reduces the financed amount. It cannot be combined with staff discounts or with the student discount. If you return the new bike within its return window, the trade-in credit is refunded as store credit, because the traded-in bike cannot be given back.
