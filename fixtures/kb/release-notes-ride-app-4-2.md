# Release Notes: Orrinby Ride App 4.2 and Halcy Firmware 2.8

Orrinby Ride app version 4.2 for iOS and Android was released on 14 September 2026, together with Halcy firmware 2.8. The app requires iOS 16 or Android 11 or later.

## Battery health screen

A new **Bike > Battery** screen shows the battery's charge cycle count, estimated health as a percentage of original capacity, and the temperature during the last charge. The health estimate is updated after every full charge.

## Charge limit

You can now set a charge limit of 80% from the app. When the bike is connected over Bluetooth during charging, the battery stops charging at 80%. The setting is available for all Halcy models with firmware 2.8.

## Auto assist mode (Halcy 52)

Firmware 2.8 adds an Auto assist mode to the Halcy 52. It adjusts motor support to the gradient and your pedalling force. Auto mode is not available on the Halcy 40, which has a cadence sensor, or on the Halcy 70 Cargo, which already has a dedicated Cargo mode.

## Improved range estimate

The range estimate now takes the last 10 rides, outside temperature and selected assist level into account. In testing, the estimate was within 10% of the actual range in 9 out of 10 rides.

## Walk assist

Walk assist on all Halcy models now starts more gently and stays at a maximum of 6 km/h. On the Halcy 70 Cargo, walk assist can now also move backwards at up to 3 km/h, which helps when manoeuvring the bike out of tight parking spots.

## Diagnostics export

The diagnostics export under **Bike > Diagnostics** now includes the last 20 stored error codes and battery cell voltages. You can share the report directly from the app with the Warranty Desk.

## Privacy

The data sharing settings have moved to **Settings > Privacy**. Ride data sharing can now be switched off without losing your ride history stored on the phone.

## Fixes

- Fixed the display showing E14 after a successful firmware update.
- Fixed Bluetooth reconnection on Android 14.
- Fixed trip distance resetting when the battery was removed.

## Known issues

On some Android phones, the firmware update may pause when the screen turns off. Keep the app open until the update finishes, which takes up to 8 minutes.
