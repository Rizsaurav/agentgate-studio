# Orrinby Care Subscriptions

Orrinby Care is our optional service subscription for bikes and e-bikes. There are three plans. Each plan covers one bike, except Care Fleet, which is priced per bike for business customers.

## Plans and prices

| Plan       | Monthly price             | Annual price                  |
| ---------- | ------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Care Basic | EUR 9 per month           | EUR 90 per year               |
| Care Plus  | EUR 19 per month          | EUR 190 per year              |
| Care Fleet | EUR 14 per bike per month | annual only, EUR 140 per bike |

Paying annually gives you two months free compared with monthly billing. Care Fleet requires at least 5 bikes and is billed annually only.

## What each plan includes

**Care Basic** includes one free annual service at a showroom or service centre, 10% off parts, and priority phone support.

**Care Plus** includes everything in Care Basic, plus two services per year, free brake pad and chain replacement, roadside assistance within 50 km of home, and theft replacement. If a bike covered by Care Plus is stolen, we replace it with the same or an equivalent model after you pay a deductible of 25% of the replacement price. A police report filed within 48 hours of the theft is required, and the bike must have been locked with a lock rated at Orrinby security level 3 or above. Theft replacement is limited to one claim per 12 months.

**Care Fleet** includes everything in Care Plus except theft replacement, and adds commercial-use warranty coverage, a dedicated account manager and a fleet dashboard in the Orrinby Fleet API.

## Starting a subscription

You can add Orrinby Care at checkout, or later from **Account > My bikes**. Care Plus can only be added within 90 days of buying the bike, because theft cover requires a recent purchase. Care Basic can be added at any time.

## Billing

Monthly plans are billed on the same day each month. If a payment fails, we retry on days 3, 5 and 7, and suspend the subscription if all retries fail. Suspended subscriptions do not include services or theft cover.

## Pausing

You can pause a monthly subscription for up to 3 months, once per calendar year, for example over winter. No fees are charged while paused, and no benefits are available. Annual and Care Fleet subscriptions cannot be paused.

## Cancelling

Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled at any time with 30 days' notice; the subscription stays active until the notice period ends. Annual subscriptions can be cancelled within the first 14 days for a full refund. After 14 days, annual plans are not refunded but you keep the benefits until the end of the year. Care Fleet contracts renew automatically and must be cancelled at least 60 days before the renewal date.

## Changing plans

Upgrading from Care Basic to Care Plus takes effect immediately and is prorated. Downgrading takes effect at the next billing date. Moving a subscription to a different bike is allowed once per year, as long as both bikes are registered to your account.
