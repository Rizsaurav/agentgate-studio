# Tessel City Bike Owner's Manual

The Tessel is Orrinby's non-electric city bicycle. It has a steel frame, a low step-through option and a maintenance-friendly drivetrain. It is the only bike in our range without a motor.

## First assembly

The Tessel ships 90% assembled. To finish assembly, turn the handlebar straight, tighten the stem bolts to 6 Nm, attach the pedals (the left pedal has a reverse thread) and fit the front wheel. Check that the quick release is closed firmly. If you are unsure, any Orrinby showroom will check the assembly for free within the first 30 days.

## Tyre pressure

Inflate the tyres to 3.5 to 4.5 bar. Lower pressure gives more comfort on cobblestones; higher pressure reduces rolling resistance. Check the pressure every two weeks.

## Gears

The 8-speed hub can be shifted while standing still, which is useful at traffic lights. Do not pedal hard while shifting. The hub oil is changed every 5,000 km, which is part of the standard service.

## Lights

The hub dynamo powers both lights whenever the wheel turns. The rear light has a standlight that stays on for about 4 minutes after you stop. No batteries are needed.

## Rack and accessories

The rear rack carries up to 20 kg and accepts Orrinby panniers. A front basket for up to 5 kg can be mounted to the head tube. A child seat can be fitted to the rear rack for children up to 22 kg, but only on the diamond frame.

## Service intervals

The first service is due after 300 km or 2 months, whichever comes first, to re-tension the spokes and adjust the cables. After that, service the Tessel every 3,000 km or once a year. Roller brakes need grease every 2 years.

## Care

Clean the bike with water and a soft brush. Wax the steel frame twice a year to prevent rust, especially in coastal areas. Store the bike indoors over winter if possible.

## Safety

Always check the brakes and lights before riding. Roller brakes have a gentler bite than disc brakes, so leave more distance when braking in the wet.
