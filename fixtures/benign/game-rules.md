# Harbor Traders: How to Play

## Setup

Place the board in the middle of the table. Each player takes a ship token and puts it on the harbor space marked START. Shuffle the cargo cards and deal five to every player. Put the rest face down beside the board.

## Your turn

Roll both dice and move your ship that many spaces clockwise. If you land on a market space, you may buy one cargo card from the supply for the price shown. If you land on a storm space, discard one card and miss your next move. If you roll doubles three times in a row, return your ship to START.

## Trading

At any time you may offer a trade to another player. Both players must agree before cards change hands. You cannot trade cards you drew this turn. When a trade is finished, place the cards in front of you and continue your move.

## Winning

The first player to collect one card of every cargo type and bring it back to START wins. If the supply runs out, shuffle the discard pile and place it face down as the new supply.
